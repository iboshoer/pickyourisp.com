Methodology

How PickYourISP plans to compare internet options when verified data is live. This page is a preview of that approach, not a live data pipeline.

PREVIEW / PLACEHOLDER / UNKNOWN: PickYourISP does not yet publish a verified coverage or plan feed. This page describes comparison intent only. No verified coverage, plan, price, speed, or address-level result is live here.

What this page is

PickYourISP is a comparison product, not an internet service provider. This page explains how we plan to compare options when live data is available, how we will cite sources, and how we will label incomplete or stale information.

How we compare

When live data exists, we plan to show side-by-side plan attributes such as technology type, sourced advertised speed and price ranges, and coverage confidence labels. Each factual claim will have a source and a checked-as-of date or retrieval timestamp when available.

Editorial views, including any future “best for” guidance, will be labeled as editorial. We will not use fabricated best seals, proprietary scores, or certified-compare badges.

Data sources when live

We plan to disclose each source when it is used:

  • FCC National Broadband Map: provider-reported information, disclosed as such.
  • ISP official public tools and plan pages: information retrieved from the provider source.
  • Research fact packs: verified claim support from research/fact-packs/ when available for a given claim.

We will not claim a proprietary inventory database or add feeds that have not been verified and documented.

Freshness

When live, our default stale thresholds are 30 days for prices and promotions and 90 days for static plan structure, unless Research sets a tighter rule. Price, speed, and coverage statements will carry a “checked as of” date or retrieved_at value.

If a source is unavailable or a fact is stale, we will remove it or label it unavailable. We will not keep an old number simply to fill an empty state.

Incomplete data policy

Incomplete information will be labeled PLACEHOLDER, UNKNOWN, EXAMPLE, or unavailable. Empty states stay honest. We will not invent an address-level availability result.

Availability tool honesty

The homepage availability tool is informational and remains a stub until a real coverage feed exists. A client-only ZIP input on this preview is not a server lookup and will not produce a fake “available at your address” result.

What we will not do

We will not invent coverage, prices, speeds, promotions, reviews, ratings, badges, awards, partner seals, or traffic. We will not imply government or ISP endorsement, or claim a live data pipeline that does not exist.
